About Us

The Silicon Wire was founded on the principle that the technology industry deserves coverage as fast-moving, over-leveraged, and loosely sourced as the industry itself. We are, as far as we can tell, the only publication willing to report a story before checking whether it happened.

Our editorial team operates under a strict policy of publishing first and, time permitting, verifying later. We believe this makes us more honest than our competitors, not less, because at least we're not pretending otherwise.
